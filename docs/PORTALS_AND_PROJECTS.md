# Reports, profile, projects and portals

Feature reference for the work added on 2026-10-14. The code is the source of truth; update this file in the same change when it drifts.

## Report downloads

`/reports/download?report=<key>&format=csv|pdf&from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD` (`src/app/(app)/reports/download/route.ts`).

| Key | Report | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| `pl`, `bs`, `cash`, `aging`, `ap`, `tax` | Profit and loss, balance sheet, cash and bank, receivables aging, payables aging, sales tax | `reports.view` |
| `leads` | Leads report (only leads the person may see) | `crm.view` |
| `workforce` | Workfolio time and activity (logged as a report view for each person) | `monitoring.view_team` |

Each report is built by `src/lib/report-tables.ts` from the same loaders as its screen, then written as CSV (spreadsheet formulas defused) or PDF (`reportPdf` in `src/lib/pdf.ts`, letterhead from company settings). Every download is audited (`report.download`). `DownloadButtons` (`src/components/ui.tsx`) adds the links.

## Workfolio

See `docs/MONITORING.md`. In short: the menu entries are Workfolio (HR and managers) and My Workfolio (everyone); a dock on every screen lets a clocked-in person share their screen, saves a snapshot every policy interval while sharing, and shows a red indicator and a countdown. Managers see **Working now** (clocked in, on break, no signal, last snapshot) but no live video. The interval is set in Settings > Monitoring Policy (5 to 120 minutes).

## My Profile

`/me/profile` has tabs: Overview, Work, Time and leave, Pay (only for people with `self.payslip` or `commissions.own`) and Access. Everything shown belongs to the signed-in person; the CNIC is their own. HR edits the record on the staff page; people update their own alternate contact, personal email, address and picture.

## Projects and tasks

- My tasks `/me/tasks` (`requireUser`, own tasks only; `src/lib/my-work.ts`, tested in `my-work.integration.test.ts`): open tasks and subtasks assigned to the person, grouped Overdue / Today / Next 7 days / Later / No due date by the company time zone, plus Done today. A tick finishes a task (and unticking reopens it as To do) through `moveTask`, so the board rules still apply (waiting-for tasks, open subtasks) and need `tasks.edit`. Each group shows 25 rows with "Show all N" (`?group=overdue` etc., 50 per page); `?show=done` lists everything finished. Rows say when a task is a subtask, has open subtasks or waits for other tasks. Tasks in cancelled or archived projects are left out, here and in the My day counts.
- `/projects` (Projects tab) and `/projects?view=tasks` (Tasks tab: assigned to me or everyone, status, priority, project, due, search; 50 per page).
  The Tasks tab edits in place (`TaskListTable.tsx`, `bulkTasksAction`, `src/lib/task-bulk.ts`, tested in `task-bulk.integration.test.ts`): the project manager (or `projects.manage`) changes assignee, priority and due date in the row; anyone who may move a task changes its status. Ticking rows opens a bar that applies one change to all of them (assign, due date or clear, priority, status, delete with a confirm step), up to 200 at a time. Each task is checked on its own: hidden projects are "not found", closed projects are skipped, assignees must be on that task's team, status follows the board rules; the result lists what was skipped and why. Delete removes subtasks too and keeps any task with logged time, work sessions, files or a content post (the same rule as removing a team member with "delete"). Each change is audited per task; bulk assigning sends the person one notification.
- Task labels (`TaskLabel`, `Task.labelIds`; `src/lib/task-labels.ts`, tested in `task-labels.integration.test.ts`): one list per organization (Copy, Design, Ads…; up to 40, names up to 24 characters, unique ignoring case, colour from the design tokens), managed in Projects → Tasks → Labels by `projects.manage` (`label-actions.ts`, audited). The project manager sets a task's labels (up to 6) in the task panel or adds and removes them in bulk from the task list; the list filters by label (`?label=`). A project's board can show its columns by status (drag to move) or by label (a task with two labels appears in both; read-only grouping). Removing a label takes it off every task in the organization. Labels are internal and never shown in the client portal. Statuses stay To do / In progress / Review / Done, because the board rules, the client portal and reports depend on them.
- Workload tab `/projects?view=workload` (`WorkloadView.tsx`, `src/lib/workload.ts`, tested in `workload.test.ts` and `workload.integration.test.ts`): shown to `projects.manage` (all projects they can see) and to anyone who manages a project (their own projects only). For each team member over the next 7 days: planned hours (estimates on open tasks overdue or due in the window) against available hours (working calendar days less public holidays and approved leave, times the full-day hours), with Over capacity (>100%), Busy (80%+), Has room, No estimates (tasks due, none estimated) or Away; plus open, overdue, due soon and hours logged this week. Opening a person (or Unassigned) lists their open tasks in the task list table, so work is handed over by changing the assignee in place or in bulk. Tasks now have an "Estimate (hours)" field (New task, the task panel, and the Hours column in the list; `parseEstimate` in `task-rules.ts`).
- Hours report, inside Workload (`/projects?view=workload&report=hours&period=week|month&at=YYYY-MM-DD`; `HoursReportView.tsx`, `WorkloadSwitch.tsx`, `src/lib/hours-report.ts`, CSV at `/projects/hours/export`, tested in `hours-report.integration.test.ts`, since 2026-11-19): the same people and projects as Workload, for any Monday-to-Sunday week or calendar month. Per person it shows: working hours, from the working calendar less public holidays and approved leave, counted only up to today in the current period; planned hours, from estimates on tasks due in the period, done or not, with the number without an estimate; and logged hours, split into client projects (the project has a client) and internal ones, with the hours still awaiting approval. Utilisation is logged against working hours so far. Totals sit on top. People who have left still show for hours they logged. A project manager without `projects.manage` sees only time on the projects they manage, and the screen says so. Clocked attendance is HR data and is not shown here.
- Planner tab `/projects?view=planner` (`PlannerView.tsx`, `PlannerBoard.tsx`, `planner-actions.ts`, `src/lib/planner.ts`, tested in `planner.integration.test.ts`): a Monday-to-Sunday week (Previous / Next / This week, `?week=` snaps to its Monday). Rows are the people on the projects in view, plus Unassigned when unassigned work is due that week; each cell lists the open tasks due that day with the day's estimates against that person's hours (full-day hours on working days, none on days off, public holidays or approved leave; red when more is due than fits). The "To plan" tray holds open tasks with no due date and overdue ones (first 60, with the total). Who sees what follows Workload: `projects.manage` and project managers see their teams and may move tasks; everyone else sees only their own row, read only. A move (drag onto a cell, or the card's Move menu for keyboard and phone) sets the due date and, on another row, the assignee in one step through `planTask`: the actor must manage the task's project, the project must be open, the task not done, and a new assignee an active staff member on that project's team (client logins are never offered). Dropping on the tray takes the due date off. Each move is audited (`task.plan`); a new assignee gets the usual assignment notice, and when someone else reschedules your task you are told the new date (`task.rescheduled`).
- Task timer (`src/lib/task-timer.ts`, `TaskTimer` table, `timer-actions.ts`, `TaskTimerDock.tsx` in the shell, `TaskTimerButton.tsx` in the task panel; tested in `task-timer.integration.test.ts`): staff on a project's team (or its manager) with `tasks.edit` press Start timer on an open task of an open project. One timer runs per person (unique on `userId`); starting another is refused with the name of the running task. While it runs, a bar under the top bar on every screen shows the task, the time so far and Stop. Stop shows the measured hours (to the minute, editable) and an optional note, and saves a `TimeEntry` through `logTime`, so the team, closed-project and 24-hours-a-day rules and the member's cost rate apply; the work date is the day the timer started in the company time zone. A timer that ran more than 10 hours needs the hours typed in. If saving is refused the timer keeps running; Discard (confirmed) removes it without saving. Deleting a task removes any timer on it. Audited as `timer.start`, `time.log` (with `fromTimer` and the measured hours) and `timer.discard`. The timer is separate from the attendance clock (`WorkSession`) and feeds Hours on projects. Not shown while viewing as someone else.
- Timeline (project page, Tasks tab; `ProjectTimeline.tsx`, `src/lib/project-timeline.ts`, `src/lib/task-schedule.ts`, `schedule-actions.ts`, tested in `project-timeline.test.ts` and `task-schedule.integration.test.ts`): each bar runs from the task's start date (`Task.startDate`, optional; when empty, the day it was created) to its due date. The project's manager or `projects.manage`, on an open project, can drag a bar to move both dates, pull its left or right end to change the start or the due date, or focus a bar and press the arrow keys (move a day; with Shift the due date, with Alt the start). Done tasks and tasks with no due date are not draggable. `scheduleTask` refuses a start after the due date, a done task, a closed project and anyone who does not manage the project; it saves but warns when the task would start before an open task it waits for is due, or when the due date passes the project deadline. Changes are audited (`task.schedule`) and the assignee is told when someone else moves their dates (`task.rescheduled`). The task panel has a Start date field with the same start-before-due rule. The weekly planner still plans by due date.
- Project templates (`ProjectTemplate`, `Project.templateId`; `src/lib/project-templates.ts`, tested in `project-templates.test.ts` and `project-templates.integration.test.ts`): Projects → Templates (`?view=templates`, `projects.manage` only) lists templates with roles, milestones, task counts and how many projects were made from each; "Add starter templates" adds Social media retainer and Ads campaign (editable). The editor `/projects/templates/[id]` (or `new`) holds team roles, milestones (due day from the project start) and up to 150 tasks (due day, hours, priority, role, milestone, labels by name, subtasks, repeat, client visible). New project → Start from a template shows a person picker per role; `createProject` copies the plan in the same transaction: people join the team with the role, their tasks are assigned to them (unfilled role = unassigned), labels are matched by name or created, due dates count from the start date (today if empty). Each person who joins hears once that they are on the team, and each assignee gets one notice with their task count. Project Settings → Save as template turns a project back into a template (roles from team roles, dates as days from the start; no client, money or files). Archive hides a template from New project; projects already made keep their tasks. All changes audited (`project_template.*`).
- Task checklists (`TaskChecklistItem`, `ChecklistTemplate`; `src/lib/task-checklists.ts`, `checklist-actions.ts`, tested in `task-checklists.integration.test.ts`): steps inside one task, lighter than subtasks (no assignee or date). The project manager (or `projects.manage`) and the task's assignee add, tick and remove items in the task panel; who ticked an item and when is kept on it. A task cannot be marked Done (board, list, bulk, My tasks) while an item is unticked, and a Done task's checklist is locked until it is reopened. A repeating task's next occurrence gets the same items unticked (and now also keeps its labels). People with `projects.manage` save a task's checklist by name ("Post QA"); anyone who can work a task adds a saved checklist in one step (repeats skipped). Saved checklists are listed and deleted on Projects → Templates. Project templates carry a checklist per task (editor "Checklist" field; starters have them on design, scheduling and ad launch tasks) and Save as template copies them. Board cards show ✓ ticked/total. Checklists are internal and never shown in the client portal. Adding, removing, applying and saving are audited.
- Project automations (`src/lib/project-automations.ts`, tested in `project-automations.integration.test.ts`; listed under Projects → Templates → "What happens automatically"): (1) a task sent to Review tells the manager; (2) the last open task of a milestone done marks the milestone done, tells the manager and, when the milestone has a billing amount and the project a client, makes one DRAFT invoice and asks `finance.post` holders to check and issue it; reopening a task of a done, uninvoiced milestone puts it back in progress; (3) a won lead whose service plan is ticked on a template ("Start automatically when a deal is won") gets its client's project from that template once, with the template's usual manager and people ("Usual people", also preselected in New project). Audited as `milestone.auto_done`, `milestone.auto_reopened` and `project.create` with `automatic: true`.
- Offboarding hand-over (`src/lib/offboarding.ts`, tested in `offboarding.integration.test.ts`): People & HR → a staff member's page → "Hand over work" (`hr.manage`) lists the open projects (Active or On hold) they manage, are on the team of, or hold open tasks in. Once the person is deactivated, exited or archived, one step gives the projects they manage to a successor (`setProjectManager`) and takes them off every open team (`removeProjectMember`), giving their open tasks to the successor (who joins with the leaver's role) or leaving them unassigned. Closed projects, finished tasks, logged time and comments keep their name. The successor and each affected project manager are told once; audited as `staff.handover`.
- Portfolio health (`src/lib/project-health.ts`, tested in `project-health.test.ts` and `project-health.integration.test.ts`): every Active project is At risk (red), Watch (amber) or On track (green); On hold shows Paused; closed projects get none. Red: deadline passed with work open, 3 or more tasks overdue or over a quarter of the open ones, or over budget. Amber: any task overdue, deadline within 7 days with under 80% done, 80% or more of the budget used, or no project update for 14 days (counted from creation when none was posted). Budget counts only for viewers with `projects.costs`; others never see it. "Today" is the company time zone date. Shown as a Health column with the reasons and a "Needs attention" card and chip (`/projects?status=ATTENTION`, red first) on the Projects list, beside "Needs attention" on the project page, and as "Projects at risk" on the home screen for people who see every project (`projects.view_all` or `projects.manage`).
- Client requests (`ClientRequest`; `src/lib/client-requests.ts`, tested in `client-requests.integration.test.ts`): on the client portal project page (`/portal/projects/[id]#requests`) a client asks for something new on an Active or On hold project (what, optional details, optional "needed by"; at most 20 waiting per project) and can withdraw it while it waits. The project manager gets a notice; the request waits on the project page Overview ("Client requests") and in Approvals. Only the project manager or `projects.manage` decides: "Accept as task" makes one client-visible task (client's words as the description, due date defaults to the date asked for, optional assignee from the team, priority) in the same transaction, so the client follows it like any shared task; "Decline" needs a reason, which the client reads. The client gets a notice either way; decisions are audited. Nothing reaches the task list without a decision.
- Weekly project emails (`src/lib/weekly-digest.ts`, job `project_weekly_digest`, tested in `weekly-digest.integration.test.ts`): Mondays from 9:00 company time. Owners (people with both `dashboard.executive` and `projects.view_all`) get "Your projects this week": active projects, at risk and to watch with the first reason each (budget reasons only with `projects.costs`), on track, tasks finished last week, overdue now, client requests waiting and client approvals waiting, with a button to `/projects?status=ATTENTION`. Each active client with an Active or On hold project gets "Your weekly project update": progress per project (as the portal counts it), tasks shared with them that were finished last week, deliverables and posts waiting for their approval, their requests, the next shared tasks due, and their project manager. It goes to the client's email, or to its portal users when the client record has none; internal tasks are never named. Both emails are editable under Settings → Email templates (`project_weekly_owner`, `project_weekly_client`); the email log (`relatedType` WeeklyDigest) makes each one go once a week.
- One Monday email for clients (2026-11-19): a client with an open project (active or on hold) gets the weekly review form inside the Monday 9:00 update instead of as a separate email. The review job (`client_weekly_reviews`) still makes the form for every reviewable client (it shows in the portal and on the Delivery tab) but leaves these clients' email to the update (`getsWeeklyUpdate`); the update issues a fresh link for last week (`reviewForUpdate`, replacing the unsent one), adds a "Your feedback" row and a "Rate last week" button under "Open your portal", records `sentTo` and `emailStatus` on the request, and is logged without the link (secret). No link when the week is already rated or its form was already emailed. Clients with only a retainer still get the review form on its own. The PM bonus rules and the form itself are unchanged.
- Proofing (`DeliverableComment`; `src/lib/proofing.ts`, tested in `proofing.integration.test.ts`): a deliverable can carry its design as a file (PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF, up to 25 MB; `StoredFile` with ownerType `DELIVERABLE` and category `v<version>`, one per version). The project manager (or `projects.manage`) attaches it to a draft on `/projects/[id]/deliverables/[did]`; a file alone is enough to send it for approval. The client opens it from Approvals (`/portal/approvals/[id]`) and comments while the version waits for them: a click on an image places a numbered pin, a PDF comment can name a page. "Request changes" then needs no note when pinned comments are open (the note says "See N comments on the design"). The team sees the pins, replies and marks comments done; each version keeps its own file and comments, and the client can't open the deliverable while the team works on a new draft. File reads follow the DELIVERABLE rule in `file-access.ts` (staff: project visibility; client: own project, never a draft). Notices go to the other side at most once a day per version.
- Project page `/projects/[id]`: header (number, status, client, manager, dates, phase; buttons New task, Log time, Post update) and key figures (progress, open and overdue tasks, next milestone, deliverables waiting on the client, profit to date for `projects.costs`), then tabs: Overview (needs attention, latest update, scope, team, coming up), Tasks (board, timeline, milestones), Updates (feed, phase, deliverables), Files (`projectAssetRights` read), Meetings (`meetings.use`), Time or Time & money (financials for `projects.costs`), Settings (status, manager, team for `projects.manage`; cost rates for `projects.costs`; archive and delete for `records.delete`, shown only once the project is Cancelled). Tabs switch in the browser (`ProjectTabs.tsx`); links use `#tab`, forms come back with `?tab=` (`tabs.ts`, older anchors like `#assets` still work). New task opens a side panel (`?new=task`).
- Team (Settings tab, `projects.manage`): add a person, edit each person's role, remove someone (`removeProjectMember` in `src/lib/project-manager.ts`). The project manager can't be removed until a new manager is chosen. When removing, the manager chooses what happens to that person's open tasks on the project: give them to another team member (who is notified), leave them unassigned, or delete them with their subtasks (tasks with logged time, work sessions, files or a content post are kept and unassigned instead); finished tasks, logged time and comments keep their name; they are notified, the change is audited (`project.remove_member`, `project.member_role`) and their project chat room closes on the next chat sync. Closed projects keep their team.
- Notifications (`src/lib/events.ts`, tested in `project-events.integration.test.ts`): added to a project; task assigned or reassigned; task sent to review or finished (to the manager and creator); project status changed (to the team); comments (assignee, creator, manager, earlier commenters); `@Name` mentions (members of the project only).
- A comment on a client-visible task can be shared with the client (checkbox). Client messages appear on the task marked "client".

## Customers and vendors

- Customer page tabs: Overview, Billing (statement, quotations, contracts), Projects, Campaigns, Portal access (people who can sign in, last sign-in).
- Vendor page tabs: Overview, Work (work orders and applications), Bills and ledger, Portal access.
- Client portal: Overview, Projects (progress, shared tasks, messages to the team), Content, Quotations, Meetings, Updates (notifications), Statement. Everything is scoped by the signed-in client; nothing takes a client id from the address.
- Vendor portal: Opportunities, Work and bills, Statement, Profile (edit email and phone only), Updates.
- Expense vouchers can name a registered vendor (optional). Posting and paying then stamp the vendor on the Accounts Payable lines, so the payout appears on the vendor's portal Statement. The payee is taken from the vendor.
- Vendors see project progress on Work and bills for projects named on their work orders or live vouchers: only project updates marked "Show to vendors" (off by default), plus the project name and phase. Nothing about the client or budget.
- Notifications sent to portal users: invoice issued, payment received, shared task comment (client); vendor bill approved, rejected or paid (vendor).

## Not built

Support tickets or chat in the portals; vendor document uploads and compliance expiry; a live video view of employees; downloads of the consolidated multi-currency reports.

## Project updates and phase
Staff post to a project's updates feed from the project page (Updates and phase). Entries marked "Show this to the client" and the current phase appear at the top of the client's portal project page (`/portal/projects/<id>`). Logic: `src/lib/project-updates.ts`; clients only ever read through `clientFeed`, which scopes by client and selects visible entries.

## Ledger downloads and manager card
Client: `/portal/statement` with `/portal/statement/pdf` and `/portal/statement/csv`. Vendor: `/vendor/ledger` with `/vendor/ledger/pdf` and `/vendor/ledger/csv`. Builders: `loadStatementDoc` and `loadVendorStatementDoc` (`src/lib/pdf-data.ts`), `statementCsv` (`src/lib/ledger-csv.ts`). The client home and project pages show the allocated project manager through `portalManager` (name and employee number only).

## SaaS support contact
SaaS clients see their support contact (`portalSupport`: the dedicated support manager, else the project manager) on the portal home, and each scheduled meeting shows its purpose. Staff set the support manager on the client's Services tab and schedule tutorial, support and inquiry calls on the Delivery tab.

## Project assets and preview links (decision 80)

`/projects/[id]#assets` (`ProjectAssets.tsx`, library in `src/lib/project-assets.ts`). Upload is a plain form post to `POST /api/projects/[id]/assets` (up to 100 MB). Public view at `/a/[token]` and files at `/a/[token]/f/[fileId]` (range requests for video). Rights: `projectAssetRights` in `src/lib/file-access.ts`. Tables `ProjectAsset` and `ProjectAssetShare`; versions are `StoredFile` rows.
