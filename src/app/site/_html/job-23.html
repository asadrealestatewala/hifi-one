BDE (Business Development Executive)

Sales and Business Development Rawalpindi Full-time
Full-time Rawalpindi On-site

Description

Operating within the Sales ; Business Development department, a Business Development Executive (BDE) drives organizational growth by identifying new market opportunities, cultivating client relationships, and securing profitable business deals. Serving as a crucial link between the company and potential clients, the BDE is tasked with building a sustainable sales pipeline and driving revenue growth.

Key Responsibilities:

  • The BDE is primarily responsible for generating qualified leads through market research, cold outreach, and professional networking. They conduct initial discovery meetings to identify prospective client needs, deliver tailored product or service pitches, draft formal proposals, and negotiate contract terms to close sales. Additionally, the BDE manages ongoing relationships with existing accounts to foster long-term retention and upsell opportunities, all while meticulously tracking sales activities and pipeline progress within a CRM system.

Requirements

  • Candidates for this role typically require a Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Marketing, or a related field, alongside one to three years of demonstrated experience in B2B sales or business development. Success in this role demands outstanding verbal and written communication skills, confident pitching abilities, strong negotiation tactics, and proficiency with modern CRM software. Ultimately, the position requires a resilient, goal-driven professional who thrives in target-oriented environments and excels at building strong business partnerships.
